What is this teaching?

Unlike Cat or Not Cat?, which has two classes, this model chooses among several classes. This is called multi-class classification. AI compares the evidence for PLAYFUL, SLEEPY, HUNGRY, and CURIOUS, then selects the strongest prediction.
