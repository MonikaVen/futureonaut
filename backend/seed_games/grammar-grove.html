Magical grammar platformer

Grammar Grove

Run through an eight-level magical forest where every word looks like the same glowing crystal. Build ladders from correct words, hunt high stars, control mistakes with nets or the red pen, and cross each glowing finish line.

💎 Identical word crystalsCorrect and incorrect forms look exactly the same until you choose one.
👾 Surprise monstersTouch a wrong form and its crystal bursts into a creature that chases you.
🕸 Trap mistakesThrow a net along the predicted arc. Trapped wrong words may still transform, but they cannot hurt you.
🪜 Build with wordsCollected correct words become ladders for climbing to hidden stars, and ladders can now stack on top of one another.
✎ Level upEarn XP and unlock a red correction pen that erases nearby wrong words.
Choose explorer:
Correct words become ladder pieces: press L/C to place one, stack ladders upward, and climb toward stars. Earn 12 XP to unlock the red pen, then press P near a wrong word.
