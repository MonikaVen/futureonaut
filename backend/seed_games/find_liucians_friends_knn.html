The algorithm measures the distance from Liucian to every labeled cat, selects the closest k cats, and predicts the class with the most votes.
prediction = majority(nearest k labels)

What is this teaching?

KNN is a simple machine-learning algorithm that learns from nearby examples. It does not create a complicated formula first—it compares a new point with known examples and lets the closest neighbors vote.
